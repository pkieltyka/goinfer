# Radeon EmbeddingGemma 2 measurement note (#39, v0.6.6)

Draft on current main `1fe41e558`. The receipts it records were measured on goinfer
**v0.6.6** `c0a5159bbff1c2fe5c8624e1399aa7438c820677`; this note does not re-measure
them. It records accepted correctness, the corpus oracle, the Q8 timestamp receipts
and the 12-cell fresh descriptive matched campaign. The separate pack-only pbrain
sync is verified below; final reviewer verdict is pending, and this is not yet
sealed. **No ratio is taken from the parked 60-cell run**, and the descriptive
paired observations are labelled descriptive, not the registered scored
comparison.

  `GOARCH=amd64`, `CGO_ENABLED=0`. The separate profile binary used for the

  `1002:7590`, 17,095,983,104 B total device memory.

  IDs and normalized 768-dimensional outputs. Inputs: Google snapshot

## Accepted correctness

- **797-position layout tests:** both Q8_0 and UD-Q6_K_XL passed with zero skips;
  4.399 s and 3.863 s; 1,315 dispatches, 235 submissions and fences, two copies and
  639,684,608 attributed bytes; zero child swap and zero new host pageout.
- **Prepared-ID tests:** Q8_0 retry passed in 8.060 s (the first attempt was void:
  host pageout rose by 55 pages after 6.984 s, 717,193,216 attributed bytes; it is
  not accepted). UD-Q6_K_XL passed in 8.155 s. Both recorded zero child swap and
  zero pageout. Same-file CPU/Vulkan 768-dimensional digests:
  Q8_0 `ca3593d8…` / `4b229a17…`; UD-Q6_K_XL `dee1d1e5…` / `0ea62a8d…`.
  CPU parity minima were cosine 0.999999999997 (Q8_0) and 0.999999999998
  (UD-Q6_K_XL) in the accepted runs.
- **2,655-position long test:** Q8_0 and UD-Q6_K_XL passed on CPU and Radeon.
  Same-file CPU/Vulkan minima were 0.9999999999997415 (Q8_0, max row-relative
  3.52e-7) and 0.9999999999998137 (UD-Q6_K_XL, 2.85e-7); zero child swap and zero
  pageout in all four cells.
- **Six fit/fidelity cells:** the six-cell fit/fidelity receipt completed its cells
  on the scoring binary with the previously qualified vector digests, zero child
  swap and zero pageout. It is the fit proof for the scoring binary; it is not a
  speed result.
- **Corpus oracle:** the pinned corpus driver built from this source and passed its
  host check: 32 documents, 107 queries, frozen prepared IDs, 13,549 positions;
  longest input `tokenizer-report`, 2,655 positions. The corpus manifest list pins
  the driver, source, fixture and token export.
