- At that same revision, the RTX passed CUDA selection and injected allocation-retry tests under both `cuda` and `cuda eg2_combined`. The Radeon RX 9060 XT with RADV Mesa 26.3.1 passed the Vulkan live test with validation enabled. An injected allocation failure before submission preserved the prepared IDs, restored the buffer count, and produced bit-identical vectors after splitting. The cgo-free co-linked test matched the requested device IDs and passed CPU parity with both CUDA selectors and Vulkan. All four focused device cells passed with zero skips. [Inventory and Metal cross-build](https://github.com/pkieltyka/goinfer/pull/60), [Exact device checks](https://github.com/pkieltyka/goinfer/pull/62)
