## Targeted native checks

- **CUDA SM 12.0.** Two independent NVIDIA ptxas 12.9.86 assemblies reproduced all six embedded images. On the NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Max-Q with driver 595.112.07, both CUDA selectors used their expected precompiled module sets and matched their PTX controls at 768 and independently normalized 512 dimensions. The pinned Unsloth Q8_0 inputs exercised singleton, packed, split, and reversed layouts. This was an untimed device proof. [#93](https://github.com/pkieltyka/goinfer/pull/93)
- **MLX Community text loader and startup.** The exact affine 8-bit checkpoint passed prepared-ID parity against same-file Go CPU and an independent MLX-VLM reference on an Apple M4. The verified downloader's canonical name and alias resolved to one cache directory without HTTP on the offline hits. Packing token rows preserved exact Go forward words and reduced logical token-table storage from 512 MiB to 144 MiB. This excludes the full table materialized by an explicit `Weights()` call. [#95](https://github.com/pkieltyka/goinfer/pull/95), [#96](https://github.com/pkieltyka/goinfer/pull/96), [#97](https://github.com/pkieltyka/goinfer/pull/97)
- **M4 staged FP32 projections.** An equivalent staged-overlay run on the frozen Q8_0 corpus measured about 2.55% higher public throughput. The exact product native run matched the singleton corpus words at both widths. Directed checks also matched Google BF16, MLX8, and Q6 at 36, 524, and 2,042 positions. [#98](https://github.com/pkieltyka/goinfer/pull/98)
- **M4 opt-in document batching.** A cgo-free native run on a base M4 with 24 GiB unified memory, macOS 27.2.1, used the same frozen 219 prepared documents and 110,157 positions per replay for Q8_0 and MLX8. Each format used five interleaved quartets and two singleton controls. All 44 replays matched their published 768 and independently normalized 512 vectors exactly. Completed commands fell from 219 to 55. The public wall measurement includes host gather, RoPE refill, waits, and pooling. [#99](https://github.com/pkieltyka/goinfer/pull/99)

| Checkpoint | Singleton median wall | Batch median wall | Median paired wall speedup | Faster pairs |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Unsloth Q8_0 | 22.733 s | 20.949 s | 1.0853× | 10/10 |
| MLX Community 8-bit | 22.753 s | 21.022 s | 1.0828× | 10/10 |

At the largest batch, 2,748 positions, the estimate and observed tracked increase were both 324,141,056 bytes.
The native proof also covered a 2,749-position singleton, allocation reuse and rollback, and retry on the same attachment.
These results apply to the explicit `metal-eg2-batch` selector on the tested M4 and prepared-ID corpus.
Existing pbrain Metal integrations use the singleton `metal` selector.

## Release preflight and decision

The release checker returned **8 of 12 done, four OWED, zero UNKNOWN** at the source commit. The pending-ledger,
standalone dependency graph, hardware matrix, branch, changelog, formatting, citation, and source CI checks were done.
The focused hardware-matrix and parity-manifest checks also passed, with 44 of 44 enforced parity-manifest families checked.
