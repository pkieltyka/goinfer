## Release checksum checks

`scripts/release_prep.py check --verify-sum` verifies the selected submodules' committed `go.sum`
hashes against Go's canonical module download for `${TAG}` outside `go.work`; it must pass at the
final pin commit. After publication, `scripts/check_release_assets.sh v0.6.5` and
`scripts/check_asset_vcs.sh` cover the Release's assets and their build stamp.
