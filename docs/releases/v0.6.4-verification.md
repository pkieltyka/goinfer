[Composed qualification](../measurements/eg2-fp16-composed-2026-11-22.md), [Product PR](https://github.com/pkieltyka/goinfer/pull/65)
- The Google and Q8_0 checkpoints each cleared 0.999 cosine against their own CPU vectors at 768 and renormalized 512 dimensions on the 81-case reference set and the fixed 200-passage slice. Their respective minimum cosines were 0.9999998162982013 and 0.9999994480482701. Singleton, packed, split, and reordered calls matched bit for bit within the composed mode. [Quality results](../measurements/eg2-fp16-composed-2026-11-22.md#composed-81-case-and-200-passage-quality-gate)
- On the 200-passage prepared-ID slice, the paired median encoder-forward speedup over `cuda-eg2-combined` was 1.86594× for Google and 1.89579× for Q8_0, with 10 of 10 faster pairs per checkpoint. Sampled peak GPU memory went from 2,130 to 1,782 MiB. The timed intervals cover encoder calls on the fixed slice. [Benchmark method, inputs, and memory](../measurements/eg2-fp16-composed-2026-11-22.md#composed-paired-abba-throughput-gate)
